Paper bags have been replacing plastic packaging in shops, restaurants, and businesses of every size for years now. That's not just the result of regulations limiting plastic use — it's also a deliberate choice by brands that want to build a responsible image and connect better with their customers.

## Sustainability that matters

Paper breaks down naturally and can be recycled multiple times, unlike many plastics. Paper bags, especially those made from [kraft](/en/eco-friendly-paper-bags) material, are fully biodegradable and are increasingly becoming the standard in environmentally conscious industries — from grocery stores to cafes and cosmetics companies.

## A bag as part of a brand's image

A paper bag printed with a logo is an advertising medium that customers are happy to reuse — on the way home, as gift wrap, or as a shopping bag. Unlike online advertising, a bag physically stays with the customer outside the store, reaching new audiences every time it's used.

Thanks to full personalization — color, size, handle type, and printing technique — a paper bag becomes part of a brand's visual identity, not just packaging.

## Durability matched to the need

Paper bags are produced in various paper weights, which lets the packaging's strength be matched to its purpose — from light bags for small purchases to sturdy [logo promotional bags](/en/custom-logo-paper-bags) meant for repeated use.

## A look for every occasion

Paper bags offer far more aesthetic freedom than plastic packaging — from elegantly finished [decorative bags](/en/decorative-paper-bags), through [wine gift bags](/en/wine-gift-bags), to seasonal [holiday collections](/en/holiday-collections). That makes it easy to match the packaging to a specific occasion, brand, and audience.
