## Materials and Technologies Used in Production

Eco-friendly paper bags are made from various types of paper, such as kraft, cellulose, or cardboard. The choice of material affects a bag's durability and stiffness. [Kraft paper](/en/eco-friendly-paper-bags) is one of the most popular materials used in paper bag production because of its strength and resistance to tearing. Thanks to this, bags made from it **can be used multiple times**, helping to reduce waste.

Modern technologies also allow for special coatings and laminates that **increase a paper bag's resistance** to moisture and mechanical damage. Solutions like these extend how long a bag can be used and allow it to carry heavier products. It's also worth noting that [printed eco-friendly paper bags](/en/eco-friendly-paper-bags) are increasingly used as an advertising medium, letting businesses promote their products in an environmentally friendly way.

## Comparison With Other Bag Types

Compared to single-use plastic bags, paper bags **have a much lower environmental impact**. Producing paper bags generates fewer CO2 emissions and uses less energy and water. Paper is also a biodegradable material — it breaks down under natural conditions without causing long-term pollution.

As for durability, **paper bags can hold their own against reusable bags made from other materials**, such as cotton or polypropylene. Of course, the durability of any given bag depends on its construction and the material used to make it. It's worth checking the technical specifications of paper bags before buying to make sure they meet your expectations.
