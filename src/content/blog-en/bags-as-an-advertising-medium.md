## Eco bags — effective advertising

An "eco" bag is a gadget made from durable material that allows for repeated use. Thanks to its properties, it's an excellent carrier for content and fits right into current trends. It's an option that lets you clearly display important elements such as text or any graphic. Because the customer uses it while going about various everyday tasks, information about our company reaches an ever-wider audience. A huge advantage is the ability to personalize it to the fullest. We choose elements like shape, size, color, handle length and width, fabric type, text colors, text content, logo, and its size. With good planning, we can stand out from the competition at a low cost while also warming up our brand's image. Studies also show that giving customers a gift — which can be a bag — has a positive effect on their attachment to us and their future purchasing choices.

## Paper bags — advertising that never dies

But when we talk about [eco-friendly bags](/en/eco-friendly-paper-bags) as an advertising medium, we don't just mean "eco" options. Paper bags, used to hand out materials about our company or additional gadgets, also play a huge role. The ability to [personalize them with a printed logo](/en/custom-logo-paper-bags) is essentially unlimited. The designs available on our website can be modified according to your preferences and needs. Paper bags reach a wide audience in a subtle but effective way, and they work great even with customers who are wary of standard advertising.
