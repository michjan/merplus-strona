## Bag colors and the character of the event

For formal occasions such as weddings or anniversaries, **muted tones** — white, silver, gold, or pastel shades — work best. They're elegant and understated, perfectly suited to such events, adding a touch of prestige. For more casual gatherings, like birthdays or company parties, [colorful paper bags](/en/decorative-paper-bags) are an excellent choice. **Bold colors** such as red, blue, or green can add energy and emphasize the festive character of the occasion. It's also worth considering **colors that match the brand**, if the bags are part of a promotional campaign — company colors can be a strong element of visual identity.

## Patterns — how to choose the right motif?

**Subtle patterns** — delicate flowers, geometric lines, or elegant ornamentation — suit more formal occasions well. For less formal events, such as company parties or holidays, you can opt for **bolder, more creative patterns**. Bags with holiday motifs, playful graphics, or unusual textures work great for themed parties or promotional campaigns. **It's important that the pattern doesn't overwhelm the whole** — it should be harmoniously matched to the color scheme and style of the event.

## Matching the bag to a brand or a person

When choosing a paper bag, it's also worth considering personalization. Paper bags can be an excellent advertising medium if designed well. For example, at company events, **a bag with the company logo or a graphic motif** tied to the business can make an interesting and practical promotional item. If instead the bag is meant to be part of a gift for someone close to you, personalizing it — for example with **a name, a dedication, or an individually chosen motif** — will make the gift even more special.
