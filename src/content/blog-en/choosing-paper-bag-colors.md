Color is one of the most important elements of a brand's visual identity. It shapes emotions, associations, and purchasing decisions. When choosing [promotional paper bags](/en/promotional-paper-bags), it's worth paying close attention to color — the right choice can strengthen a company's image, while the wrong one can create unnecessary dissonance.

## Color as a Brand Identity Element

A printed paper bag is often a customer's first physical contact with a brand after making a purchase. That's why its color should match the brand's visual guidelines and the colors used in the logo, on the website, and in other marketing materials. Consistency in color builds recognition and a professional image.

## The Psychology of Color in Packaging

Every color carries a specific meaning and triggers particular emotions:

- **Red** — energy, dynamism, often used in the food and hospitality industry.
- **Green** — nature, ecology, health — ideal for eco brands and natural products.
- **Blue** — trust, professionalism, often chosen by service-sector companies.
- **Black and gold** — elegance, prestige — work great for premium brands.
- **Pastels** — softness, subtlety — popular in the cosmetics industry and for children's products.

## Natural Kraft Paper vs. Colorful Prints

Bags made from natural [kraft paper](/en/eco-friendly-paper-bags) are gaining popularity for their eco-friendly character and raw, natural look. They provide an excellent backdrop for colorful prints, which become even more striking through the contrast.

## How to Choose a Bag Color for Your Industry?

When choosing a color scheme, it's worth considering not just aesthetics, but also how the color will work with the product inside the bag and what emotions you want to evoke in the customer. A well-designed bag should be consistent with your overall visual identity — from the logo, through the website, to the packaging.
