## Original Packaging for the Busy

People who don't have the time, inclination, or simply don't enjoy handmade crafts can, of course, choose a **gift bag with a personalized print** that ties into the gift inside or suits the recipient's taste. If the priority is a more eco-friendly yet still distinctive option, [corrugated cardboard bags](/en/corrugated-cardboard-products) make an ideal choice. They have an original yet neat look, are elegant, and highly versatile. And as befits [eco-friendly bags](/en/eco-friendly-paper-bags), they can later be reused for everyday purposes — not just as gift wrapping.

## DIY Gift Wrapping

People with an artistic streak can choose a gift bag sized to fit the present, in a single solid color, to decorate themselves in whatever way they like. [Corrugated cardboard bags](/en/corrugated-cardboard-products) work well here too, as do [brown kraft paper bags](/en/eco-friendly-paper-bags). When it comes to decorating a bag, imagination is the only limit: there's a wide range of paper crafts and art supplies available to create your own decorations. Paints, markers, cut-outs, decorative ribbons, stickers, pom-poms, and glitter are all great options. Decorating bags is also a wonderful activity to do together with kids, sparking their creativity — and a personalized wrapping like this will be appreciated by anyone, regardless of age or occasion.
