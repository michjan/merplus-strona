## Custom promotional bags: when are they worth considering?

Printed paper bags are a product that works very well as a form of advertising. They're very often used in clothing stores, grocery stores, and lately even in supermarkets. They've become a great alternative to plastic bags. **They stand out for their very good quality while also being eco-friendly, something customers really appreciate.** Depending on our needs, [promotional bags can carry a logo](/en/custom-logo-paper-bags), a name, or a color that identifies a particular company. Promotional bags are manufactured by Mer Plus, a company that guarantees the highest quality materials. We offer a wide selection of products in terms of both look and color. All bags are suitable for reuse. We make gift packaging as well as various kinds of bags for promotional campaigns. It has to be said that bags like these have become highly sought-after across many industries.

## Laminated paper bags

Laminated paper bags have become a very prestigious product among paper bags in general. **They're coated with a layer of glossy or matte film, which also protects the product from damage.** They're most often used for gift wrapping. They're also popular with businesses selling high-end products. Because they're made from very durable materials, they can be reused, so they'll serve customers and business partners for a long time. **Because of their design, they're often ordered by companies for use at events, all kinds of conferences, or training sessions.** Cotton cords or satin ribbons in various colors are typically used with this kind of bag. They're highly sought after by the pharmaceutical, cosmetics, and clothing industries, and they suit any occasion.
