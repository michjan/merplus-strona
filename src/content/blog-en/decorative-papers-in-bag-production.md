## Decorative papers as the base for a promotional bag

[Promotional bags](/en/promotional-paper-bags) can be made from many different materials. The most popular is thick paper in a single, solid color. But if you want something distinctive that stands out from other bags and catches the eye, it's worth considering **making promotional bags from [decorative papers](/en/decorative-paper-bags)**. This option achieves an exclusive, elegant effect with a one-of-a-kind design. A promotional bag made from decorative paper can be a great complement to a brand's advertising and promotional efforts, something even the most demanding customers will appreciate.

What decorative papers are used to produce promotional bags? They can include, for example, **mass-colored, textured, coated, or pearlescent papers**. Combined with an elegant print in the form of a logo or advertising slogan, a bag like this will make even passersby take notice of the brand and remember it more easily. Choose the **decorative paper, its color and texture, and then the type of print and handles** (cords or elegant ribbons), and if you want something truly exceptional, **go for artistic hand decoration** and surprise everyone with a genuinely striking promotional bag.
