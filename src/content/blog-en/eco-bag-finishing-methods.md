## How are paper bags made?

Printed bags are among the most popular kinds of paper bags. Their production begins with preparing a design based on the client's specifications. **Once the design, worked out together with our graphic designers, is ready, photopolymer printing plates can be used.** Printing on an eco paper bag can be done using:

- flexography,
- offset,
- screen printing.

Which method is chosen depends on the type of printed bag. If the print is meant to cover the entire surface of the bag, flexography is the best choice. Flexography is also the most suitable method **for large orders, above three thousand pieces.**

Offset is used to produce [eco paper bags](/en/eco-friendly-paper-bags) printed in a **central area**. What's more, a print made this way can be further embellished — for example by protecting it with matte or glossy film, UV varnish, raised printing, or hot foil stamping. Offset also allows for printing both smaller and larger quantities of bags.

## What are the finishing methods for eco-friendly bags?

Our range includes many different types of [eco-friendly bags](/en/eco-friendly-paper-bags), finished in a variety of ways. One of our offerings is biodegradable bags made from ECO KRAFT paper, produced from recycled paper and virgin fibers. They were created with customers in mind who value elegance while also prioritizing sustainability. ECO KRAFT bags can be personalized with patterns, prints, or a logo, and paired with eco-friendly handles made from cotton or jute cord. They can be finished **using offset printing, screen printing, hot stamping, and embossing or debossing**. Another finishing option for eco bags is adding handles made from cotton cord in any color, natural brown jute cord, or satin and grosgrain ribbons. Punching eyelets around the handle holes is also possible.

Another one of our offerings is eco-friendly paper bags made from **[decorative papers](/en/decorative-paper-bags) that are mass-colored, textured, pearlescent, and coated.** These can be finished not only with offset or screen printing, but also with regular or raised UV varnish, along with handles in the form of various cords, ribbons, or die-cut handles. We also offer hand decoration, done by our experienced artists using paints, glitter, and adhesives.

Our range also includes printed promotional bags as well as [solid-color bags](/en/solid-color-paper-bags).
