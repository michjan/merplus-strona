## The smallest gift bag sizes — ideal for small presents

Small presents, such as jewelry, cosmetics, or CDs, need packaging that highlights their elegance while staying functional. The smallest [paper gift bags](/en/gift-bags), such as **T Micro** (65x100x50 mm) or **T Mini** (100x100x65 mm), work great for small gifts. Compact bag sizes make sure the gift doesn't get "lost" inside the packaging, while the whole thing still looks attractive and elegant.

Another popular size is **T CD** (170x170x65 mm) and **T CD Max** (200x230x130 mm), ideal for wrapping discs, books, or small gadgets. Thanks to their square shape, these **gift bags** are a great fit for presents with more regular shapes, making wrapping easy and giving a stylish look.

## Medium and large gift bags — versatile solutions for bigger presents

When you're giving a bigger gift — a bottle of wine, a cosmetics set, or even clothing — it's worth choosing a medium or large gift bag. Sizes like **T Kon** (135x340x95 mm), **T4 Max** (260x340x120 mm), or **T5** (330x500x150 mm) offer enough room for more substantial gifts. In particular, **gift bags** in the **T Kon Max** (160x370x120 mm) and **T Vodka** (110x380x90 mm) formats are often used for packaging bottles of alcohol, providing both safety and elegance.

For even bigger gifts, models like **T Max** (730x490x160 mm) or **T8** (475x365x180 mm) are an excellent choice, offering plenty of room for unusual and large items. Such bags are ideal for special occasions where the size and durability of the packaging really matter. It's worth remembering that choosing the right [gift bag size](/en/solid-color-paper-bags) not only makes transport easier but also highlights the significance of the gift being given.
