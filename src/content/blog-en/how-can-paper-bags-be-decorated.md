## Paper bag decoration techniques

One of the most popular materials for making paper bags is [kraft paper](/en/eco-friendly-paper-bags). Kraft paper bags stand out for their durability and natural look, making them an ideal canvas for decoration. The most commonly used techniques include:

- **printing** — done using various methods such as screen printing, offset, or digital printing, allowing high-quality graphics and text on the bag's surface;
- **embossing** — gives bags an elegant, three-dimensional effect, ideal for more exclusive products;
- **hand decoration of paper bags** — bags can be painted, drawn on, or decorated using the decoupage technique.

Hand-decorated and embossed [decorative paper bags](/en/decorative-paper-bags) are often chosen for special occasions such as weddings, birthdays, or holidays, where personalization and a distinctive look are especially valued.

## Add-ons and accessories for paper bags

Beyond decoration directly on the paper, paper bags can be enhanced with various add-ons and accessories. **Bags with cord handles** are not just practical but elegant too. The cord can be made from different materials, such as cotton, jute, or polyester, and comes in a wide range of colors. Adding a cord to a bag not only boosts its durability but also adds aesthetic appeal and functionality.

Another popular add-on is **ribbons and bows**. Decorative paper bags can be tied with colorful ribbons, giving them a festive or luxurious look. Other accessories, such as **metal handles, clasps, or magnets**, can also be used to boost the functionality and appeal of paper bags. Properly decorated paper bags not only catch the eye — they also underscore the unique character of a brand or occasion.
