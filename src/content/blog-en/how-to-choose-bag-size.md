## What Makes Paper Bags a Good Choice?

Decorative paper bags are widely used for gift wrapping, but most businesses order them printed for product promotion. They're practical and elegant, which makes them an excellent promotional item — though the graphic design matters just as much as the bag itself. Every product typically carries a logo, but also a tagline, address, website, and similar details. Paper bags are reusable, functional, aesthetically pleasing, and durable, which extends the life of a brand's advertising campaign. They reliably build a positive image for the advertiser. At MER Plus, we put real effort into making sure every order has an outstanding design — every detail counts toward an effect that catches the recipient's eye and, ultimately, drives sales.

## How to Choose the Right Bag Size?

[Promotional paper bags](/en/promotional-paper-bags) are used at events and special occasions, and also for packing everyday purchases — so their applications are broad. They're made from coated, eco-friendly, offset, laminated, and single-color paper in various shades. If the bag is meant for a specific group of clients, it should be made from the highest-quality material. **On our site you can choose the right packaging size using our [size guide](/en/size-guide)** database of ready-made die-cuts. This saves time and the cost of producing a new die-cut. **If you don't find the right dimensions in the table, you can order a different die-cut at an affordable price.** Advertisers often look for a non-standard bag size, perfectly matched to the product being packed.
