## Choosing the Packaging

The foundation of a well-wrapped bottle of alcohol is choosing the right packaging. [Paper bags for wine and spirits](/en/wine-gift-bags) are not only an eco-friendly but also an elegant option that works perfectly for this purpose. Available in various sizes and patterns, they can be matched to the occasion and to individual preferences. When choosing a bag, it's worth paying attention to its durability — thicker paper offers better protection for the bottle. An added benefit is **the ability to personalize the bag, for example with a printed message or dedication**, which makes the gift feel even more personal.

Besides traditional bags, it's also worth considering special **boxes for alcohol**. These are usually made from sturdier cardboard, often with additional compartments and protective inserts — especially useful when shipping a gift over a distance.

## Extras and Decorations

Packing a bottle well isn't just about choosing a bag or box — it's also about attention to detail, such as extras and decorations. **Ribbons, cords, message tags**, or small additions like a **corkscrew or decorative glasses** can significantly elevate a gift's aesthetic appeal. When packing, it's also worth thinking about protection — for more delicate bottles like champagne or certain wines, **using extra cushioning materials** (tissue paper or paper filler) helps protect the gift from damage.

A well-wrapped bottle isn't just a sign of respect for the recipient — it also shows the giver's attention to detail. Whatever method you choose, what matters is that the wrapping reflects the recipient's taste and personality. That way, the gift will be both beautiful and full of personal character.
