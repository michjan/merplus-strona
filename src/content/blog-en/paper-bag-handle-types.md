## Traditional Cord Handles

Cord is by far the most common type of paper bag handle. Its advantage is durability and versatility — it can match the bag's color or contrast with it. **The cord can also be made from synthetic or natural materials, mostly cotton.** Its length is always matched to the size of the bag, which is why a corded paper bag is comfortable to carry, with the handle sitting well in the hand.

## Elegant Ribbons

Our [high-quality paper bags](/en/gift-bags) can also be finished with an **elegant, tasteful satin or grosgrain ribbon**. Bags like these work perfectly as gift packaging, since they're a refined accessory in their own right. The recipient can reuse them afterward for their own needs or for storing small items. Ribbons are matched to the bag's color, so the whole piece looks cohesive.

## Handles Cut Into the Bag

An alternative to cords and ribbons is a handle hidden within the bag itself — cut directly out of the paper. It can come in various sizes and shapes, and regardless of size it sits comfortably in the hand, making the bag easy to carry. It's worth noting that thanks to **reinforced top edges**, this type of handle works well for **bags made from paper of any weight and format**.
