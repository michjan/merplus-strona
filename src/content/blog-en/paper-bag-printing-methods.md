## Printing: What's Worth Knowing?

The first step to getting an elegant print on your bag is creating a precise design. As the brand owner, you know best what your vision is and what the artwork should include. Still, it's worth having the final version reviewed by a professional, who will make sure the elements are laid out well and the colors are chosen properly. With the right guidance, your design becomes clean, striking, and memorable.

The final result is also shaped by the material the artwork is printed on. Every paper has its own characteristics, which affect both the product's durability and the printing options available. It's worth thinking in advance about which finishing touches you want to use, and what details will enhance your [bags](/en/eco-friendly-paper-bags).

## Screen Printing

The first of the core methods used for paper bags is screen printing. It's mainly used for simple prints that don't require more than five colors, and it also works well for short print runs.

How does this method create a print? A special mesh is used, together with ink, to transfer the graphic onto the paper. The mesh is made from various types of fibers, and its structure lets the printing process be matched to how detailed the design is — allowing very fine mesh openings that reproduce even the smallest details. The whole process is controlled by precisely programmed equipment.

## Flexographic Printing

The second popular option is flexographic printing, which works well when your design is more detailed and complex. This method is also recommended when:

- the artwork involves tonal gradients,
- the design covers a large surface area,
- you're producing a large print run.

This method allows for up to ten colors. Its key advantages are high-quality output and efficient production.
