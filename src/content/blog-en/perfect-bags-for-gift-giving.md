## A good gift bag — better than the best box

You could argue for hours about which came first, the chicken or the egg. The same could be said for debating what's better: a box or a gift bag. Luckily, when it comes to bags, they have one important feature that lets them beat every other wrapping method — boxes included. That feature is handles. It's the only form of gift protection that doesn't leave the recipient in the awkward position of wondering how to carry the present home. The huge range of colors and styles gives people a way to express themselves and highlight the magic of the moment. That's possible thanks to another feature boxes simply don't have, which is why they lose across the board. **Our [decorative bags](/en/decorative-paper-bags) aren't just elegant and durable — they can also be enriched with any text or graphic**, using offset printing (CMYK, Pantone), screen printing, hot stamping (gold, silver), or embossing/debossing. This way they not only highlight the magic of the moment and stay memorable longer, they can also become an ideal advertising medium.

## Paper gift bags for businesses

The exclusive [promotional bags](/en/promotional-paper-bags) we offer answer the needs of modern businesses. As mentioned above, **they're an ideal advertising medium and a way to promote a brand. The nearly endless possibilities of printing techniques and paper finishing mean every bag becomes a gadget in its own right — one that sticks in the consumer's memory, builds the company's image perfectly, and helps "beat the competition."** An advantage of good gift bags is that they can be produced in any unusual size, which significantly shortens gift-wrapping time while always delivering the same high-quality result. Looking for a way to accentuate a mini gift? We have tiny bags sized 5 x 7 cm for you. Have a sizeable gift to wrap? Decorative packaging in dimensions of 73 x 58 cm awaits. Need a different, unusual [die-cut](/en/size-guide)? No problem. We make sure your gifts always look tasteful, elegant, and accented appropriately for the occasion. Beyond decoration in the form of printed text or images, we can shape customer taste and perception through the right choice of handles. And the choice there is far from ordinary. That role can be played by cotton cord, natural brown jute cord, satin ribbon, grosgrain ribbon, or a die-cut handle.

## Gift bags that care about the natural environment

Finally, one more advantage that might convince anyone weighing paper versus bag. Our bags also answer the needs of those looking for eco-friendly solutions. We produce [gift bags](/en/gift-bags) in Eco Kraft, made from white and brown kraft paper, as well as ribbed and smooth varieties. Available weights range from 100 g/m² to 250 g/m², making it easy to match them to the bag's contents. We make them from recycled paper and virgin fibers, so on one hand they're durable and fulfill their function perfectly, and on the other they break down quickly. As you can see, if you're looking for a fashionable, proven way to wrap a gift and present your business, our gift bags are exactly what you need.
