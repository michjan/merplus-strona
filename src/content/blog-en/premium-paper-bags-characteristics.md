## What Sets Them Apart?

What makes [premium promotional paper bags](/en/premium-paper-bags) stand out? First and foremost, the material they're made from. Our main raw material is the trusted, highly regarded GARDA coated paper, known for its smooth texture, excellent whiteness, and 150–300 g weight. Using it gives every bag a touch of elegance while keeping it sturdy and appropriately rigid. Beyond the paper itself, we also use the latest technology and top-tier equipment throughout the process. All of this lets us achieve an outstanding result that often exceeds initial expectations. Your design is reproduced on the bag down to the smallest detail, with excellent quality and rich, saturated colors throughout. The final print can take many forms, depending on your needs — the most common elements on premium bags are a company logo, company name, or artwork created specifically for an event.

## Modern Printing Methods

We achieve outstanding final results partly through professional, innovative printing methods. We primarily use offset printing as well as screen printing. But we don't stop there — we also use spot UV varnish, which gives a bag entirely new properties. Even at the design stage, you can decide which elements should be highlighted with an elegant sheen. In our print shop, we also use raised UV varnish. What does that mean? This varnish contains a special agent that causes the chosen element to increase in volume as it dries and cures. That makes the spot raised and perfect for highlighting important elements like a logo or ornament. **We also use innovative hot stamping when creating premium bags.** This method is based on hot foil stamping, which produces an outstanding visual effect. Hot stamping is usually done in elegant gold and silver tones, though other colors are available depending on client preference. Embossing — both recessed and raised — has also been a popular choice for years, adding elegance and genuinely earning the "premium" label.

## Finishing Touches — A Way to Stand Out

If you're looking for a product that will truly set you apart from the rest, try our range of finishing options. Why? Various technological processes give the material new properties and an original look. Finishing touches make every project more prestigious, and [promotional paper bags](/en/promotional-paper-bags) that catch the customer's eye. What can you choose from? We recommend glossy and matte foil, which both strengthen and enrich the surface. In our print shop we also use hybrid varnish — drip-off varnish — which has grown more popular every year. It creates an entirely new style that combines a glossy and matte effect. Other available options include holographic foil, polyester foil, and even hand-applied decorations.

## Available Handle Types

Still, many elements come together to create an outstanding final result. While the paper surface usually plays the main role, the extra details matter too. Each one should be chosen to combine aesthetics with durability and fit the overall composition. We've prepared a range of handle types for our clients that ensure comfortable carrying while staying elegant. The most commonly chosen options remain the classic cotton cord and elegant, multi-colored ribbons. A recently popular option is the so-called die-cut handle — an opening cut directly into the bag. Thanks to attention to every detail, our bags impress and become a company's recognizable trademark.
