## What Counts as Eco-Friendly Packaging?

Packaging that can be called eco-friendly is made entirely from natural raw materials. The production process matters too: it should be as simple as possible and emit minimal gases, so it has little to no impact on the environment. Eco-friendly packaging is usually reusable, and once it's worn out beyond further use, it can be recycled to make the most of every available resource. Cardboard boxes and paper bags are the most commonly used forms of eco-friendly packaging. An interesting, less obvious option is [promotional bags made from corrugated cardboard](/en/corrugated-cardboard-products), which are noticeably sturdier than standard brown-paper eco bags and work well for marketing purposes.

## The Benefits of Using Eco-Friendly Boxes and Bags

The biggest advantage of eco-friendly packaging is, of course, its minimal impact on the environment — that's its main purpose. But that's not the only benefit: the ability to recycle paper and cardboard means lower costs, which translates into lower electricity and water bills for manufacturers, and lower prices for consumers. Using eco-friendly packaging is also often a way to build a positive brand image, since more and more customers prefer to shop at stores that care about the environment. Eco-friendly boxes and bags really are a win on every front.
