## What Qualities Do Elegant Paper Bags Have?

Elegant paper bags are bags that impress with their look and quality. They tend to share the following traits:

- **They're made from high-quality coated or laminated paper**, weighing 150 to 250 g/m². This paper is smooth and glossy, giving the bags an elegant, luxurious character perfectly suited to a wide range of occasions.
- **They're decorated using advanced printing techniques**, such as offset, screen printing, flexography, or digital printing. This makes it possible to add any design, logo, or text while keeping the highest quality and color accuracy — strengthening the message a brand wants to send its audience.
- **They're finished with special varnishing, embossing, or foiling techniques**. These can create a matte, glossy, metallic, or holographic effect, adding extra shine and prestige and making the bags suitable for truly special occasions.
- **They come with sturdy, elegant handles** made from cotton tape, polypropylene, or cord. Dedicated handles can further highlight a bag's elegant character.
