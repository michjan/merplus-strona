## A fashionable and practical item

[Eco-friendly bags](/en/eco-friendly-paper-bags) are hugely popular. Consumer eco-awareness keeps growing year after year. **Eco bags made of paper contain no artificial substances,** which benefits our environment and the recycling process. Biodegradable bags are reusable, which is why we often reach for them when going shopping, skipping plastic bags altogether. That matters a great deal, since plastic bags harm the environment and can take up to 700 years to break down.

Paper bags are a very luxurious, elegant, and durable accessory. Most premium brands deliberately choose to invest in paper bags. Their modern design, various die-cuts, format, size, and capacity make paper bags an ideal item for both a small token gift and a bigger present. It's worth mentioning that paper bags can also be personalized, which means the range of printing options is enormous. They look perfect as a bag holding clothing or a corporate gift for a client.

There's no shortage of printing options on eco paper. **The most popular paper used to produce eco bags is [kraft paper](/en/eco-friendly-paper-bags).** Safe, durable, and hand-finished, it will certainly look original while staying eco-friendly.

## Can eco-friendly bags be an advertising medium?

Of course they can. With paper bags, we can raise customer awareness, build brand attachment, and above all boost our brand's recognition. By adding our logo or an interesting graphic, eco paper bags become a great gadget and advertising medium.
