## Biodegradable Bags

Eco bags are one answer to the growing pile of waste and the trouble with disposing of plastic packaging. Plastic, while very convenient to use, is a substance that pollutes the natural environment on a massive scale. We see discarded plastic bags in our forests and rivers. Fragments of plastic end up in animals' bodies, and are sometimes even present in some of the food we eat.

[Eco-friendly bags](/en/eco-friendly-paper-bags) are usually **fully biodegradable**. That means they're made from material that, left in a natural setting, breaks down on its own under the influence of weather conditions and microorganisms. A bag like this can therefore be **disposed of easily** and stops being any kind of problem within a short time. Biodegradable eco bags are **made from paper**, which comes from **natural cellulose** — the basic building block of wood. An added advantage of bags like these is that the raw material used to make them comes **from recycled paper**. Eco-friendly bags are therefore a good example of an environmentally friendly circular economy, where all goods are made **from recycled materials** and are themselves either biodegradable or suitable for further processing and reuse.

Eco bags aren't ordinary paper bags. Their production uses paper made **without chlorine** and without the strongly concentrated, hazardous, hard-to-dispose-of **acids** used elsewhere. Thanks to this technology, it's not just the product itself that's safe for the environment, but the way it's produced too.
